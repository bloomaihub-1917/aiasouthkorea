01 — ABOUT

About the Symposium

The 2026 East Asia Symposium takes up two questions through the lens of Seoul: how to reuse existing buildings and respond to demographic change, and how physical construction and virtual practice coexist in the digital era. It brings together an international network of architects to share Seoul's urban and architectural experience.

Pair 01

Buildings and/or Bodies

Reuse and renovation of existing buildings, and architecture in an age of demographic change.

Pair 02

Building and/or Not Building

Physical architectural practice, virtual space, and speculative practice in the digital era.
